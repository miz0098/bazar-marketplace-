# BAZAR Business Rules V1 — Phase F19

Version: **1.0**, 2026-10-27 UTC. Business authority: the explicit Phase F19 user instruction in this session; not inferred from earlier code, tests or Shopify. Base: merged PR #29, `2057ecbb0e40b8866cf73a179e6153ecdd8110e9`. Branch: `codex/phase-f19-business-rules`.

This is the canonical **target business policy**, not an assertion that it is implemented. Approval is limited to what the user explicitly approved. Stable BR IDs are never reassigned; future edits increment version with supersession/history. “Approved” does not approve a provider, technical proposal, missing rounding rule or legal conclusion. No new functional coverage and no Demo 100% / Full Audit Ready claim.

Companions: [domain contracts](COMMERCE_DOMAIN_CONTRACTS.md), [decision register](BUSINESS_DECISION_REGISTER.md), [implementation contract](F20_F29_IMPLEMENTATION_CONTRACT.md), [historical F18 evidence](FULL_MARKETPLACE_READINESS_AUDIT.md).

## Policy precedence and unchanged boundaries

F19 target rules supersede earlier *unresolved policy assumptions* on single-code scope, binary availability/no inventory, cart completion, cancellation, deadlines, carrier pricing and commission. Historical ADRs/tests/migrations remain true descriptions of their isolated implementations. They are not silently reinterpreted as compliant V1. See [conflict inventory](COMMERCE_DOMAIN_CONTRACTS.md#existing-implementation-conflicts-and-migration-boundary).

One active authenticated cart/user, local guest cart and no automatic merge remain approved F9 rules. No guest wishlist merge rule is invented. Existing UAH integer minor-unit contracts remain the technical baseline; display precision is not permission to select financial rounding. Purchased quantity is retained for cart/returns arithmetic, not stock tracking. “Persist until removed” never means indefinite lawful retention or prevention of approved purchased-cart clearing.

No new profile menu or «Огляд» item, no admin «Клієнти» module, no hidden customer directory. UI parity stays frozen; money/contact/error/accessibility changes in later phases must reuse approved layout.
