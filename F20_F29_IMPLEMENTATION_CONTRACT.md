## Financial and privacy release gates

1. No fractional or multi-line financial activation before PD-01 approved expected examples and per-unit/seller reconciliation. Do not hide unapproved policy in an algorithm library.
2. No zero-payable completion until PD-04 defines non-payment proof. Do not send zero “successful payment” or treat cap as rejection of the approved promo maximum.
3. No quantitative inventory reservation/decrement or synthetic carrier tariff requirements introduced by old F18 backlog wording.
4. No direct-cancel/system-cancel financial success without proper distinct provider evidence; legal entitlement and commission preference remain separate.
5. No real PII, employee grant, outbound notification, carrier/shipment/payment provider action merely because a type/migration/test exists.
